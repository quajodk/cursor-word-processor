# Coastal Erosion Monitoring

The survey团 has completed its second season of measurement along the eastern shoreline.
This interim report summarises findings and sets out the working group's recommendations.

## Method

Measurements were taken at 14 fixed transects at monthly intervals. Each transect was
surveyed using differential GPS, with a vertical accuracy of ±2cm.

> The 2024 baseline remains the reference datum for all comparisons in this report.

### Instrumentation

| Instrument | Count | Accuracy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Differential GPS | 3 | ±2 cm |
| Tide gauge | 2 | ±1 cm |
| Sediment trap | 14 | n/a |

## Findings

1. Mean shoreline retreat was **1.4 m** across all transects.
2. Retreat was concentrated in the northern sector.
3. Sediment budget remains *negative* for the third consecutive year.

The northern sector is of particular concern. Retreat there exceeded 3 m at two transects,
and the dune toe has migrated landward past the 2019 marker at transect 7.
